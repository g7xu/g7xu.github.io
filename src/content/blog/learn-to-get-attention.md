While your technical skill is extremely important, since it shows whether you can create value for others, the ability to gain attention from others is just as important. Pitching yourself, building a personal brand, creating online visibility, and attending events earn you trust from others, one of the most important assets we have in society. This is what brings more opportunities when you are among people with similar skill levels.
