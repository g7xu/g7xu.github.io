This domain is about deliberately building an online public figure, but a long-term presence across the platforms where the right people (recruiters, peers, collaborators, an audience) already are.

## The two axes

Every platform reduces to the same two levers:

1. **Presence** — the content and positioning that makes you findable and credible *before* you reach out. Your profile, what you publish, and how consistently you show up. This is what people check the moment your name appears.
2. **Networking** — the relationships and outreach that turn presence into actual connections and opportunities. Who you reach out to, how warm the approach is, and how you follow up.

The two compound: a strong presence makes outreach land, and outreach drives people back to your presence. Neither works well alone.

## Cross-platform principles

These hold regardless of platform:

- **Quality over quantity.** A relevant, engaged network amplifies you; a random one dilutes you. See [[LinkedIn Networking]].
- **Relationships, not transactions.** Lead with what the other person finds relevant, not with the ask.
- **Warm beats cold.** Use signals (shared context, engagement, events, mutual ties) to earn a reason to connect before the first message.
- **The strength of weak ties** — most opportunities come from acquaintances, not close friends. See [[The strength of weak ties]].
